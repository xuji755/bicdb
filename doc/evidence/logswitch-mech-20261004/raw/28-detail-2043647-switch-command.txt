  ── 描述 ──
  WAITEVENT: V8 "switch logfile command" WaitEvent: switch logfile command Identifier: kcrfsus
  Registered in: kcrfh.h (8.0.3) Definition: Waiting for log switch to complete following a switch
  logfile command. Wait Time: We wait until the log switches but timeout every 5 seconds to check for
  interrupts etc.. Parameters: No parameters Commentary: If it is taking a long time to switch logs it
  is sensible to check the alert log and check what LGWR is doing. Related: <WaitEvents:Log> .

  ── 摘要 ──
  switch logfile command 等待事件表示会话执行 ALTER SYSTEM SWITCH LOGFILE 命令后等待日志切换完成，内部标识符为 kcrfsus，每 5 秒超时检查中断。若等待时间过长，通常反映日志切换性能瓶颈，应检查告警日志和 LGWR 活动，关联 WaitEvent:L。
